Language Reader

Sending text

Right-click selected text and choose Send selection to Language Reader, or right-click elsewhere on a page to send the whole article.

The toolbar button lets you check the text, title and language before sending.

Shortcut: Alt+Shift+L sends the selection, or the whole article when nothing is selected. Change it under Manage Extension Shortcuts on the add-ons page.

Saving asks Firefox for permission to reach your server's address. The add-on sends text only when you ask it to, and only to that server.
